# Sample Remote Work Handbook

This is a small demonstration document for the RAG chatbot. It is not a real company policy.

## 1. Purpose

This handbook explains the remote-work, travel, and device-security rules for the sample
organization. It applies to employees and contractors unless a section says otherwise.

## 2. Eligibility

Full-time employees become eligible for remote work after 90 calendar days of continuous
employment. Contractors are not eligible for remote work under this policy.

## 3. Core Collaboration Hours

Remote employees must be online from 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday through Thursday.
Outside those hours, schedules may be arranged with the employee's manager.

## 4. Equipment Stipend

Eligible employees receive an equipment stipend of $600 per calendar year. Itemized receipts
are due within 30 days of purchase.

## 5. Workspace Requirements

Remote employees need a private workspace with a reliable internet connection and a working
camera for video meetings.

## 6. Working From Another Country

Employees may work from another country for no more than 20 business days per calendar year.
Written approval from the manager and People Operations is required at least 30 days in advance.

## 7. Business Travel

For business travel, the hotel limit is $180 per night in Tier 1 cities. Other cities follow
the standard travel schedule. Keep itemized receipts for every reimbursable expense.

## 8. Device Security

Report a lost or stolen phone to the Security team within 24 hours. Lock the device remotely
as soon as you notice it is missing. Do not store confidential documents on personal devices.

## 9. Questions

Contact People Operations for questions about eligibility or leave. Contact the Security team
for lost devices or suspected account compromise.
